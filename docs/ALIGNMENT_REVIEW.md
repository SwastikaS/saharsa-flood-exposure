# Visual alignment screening

Reviewed native LULC raster with NRSC inundation outlines and SOI block boundaries in Mahishi, Nauhatta and Salkhua. The overlays occupy the expected landscape and no gross coordinate-system displacement is apparent. This is not a measured registration test or independent classification validation.

The LULC valid-data boundary differs visibly from the SOI administrative outline, especially in Nauhatta. Consequently 1.292437 km² of mapped district inundation has no LULC classification. This was excluded from class assignments and retained as unknown, not treated as dry or non-cropland.

50.698516 km² intersects baseline crop classes 2–5. Of that, 25.979916 km² is classified as Rabi crop in the 2024–25 baseline; do not describe the full overlap as standing September crops or crop damage. Source mapping scale 1:250,000 limits small-area precision. The appropriate interpretation is district/block screening of official mapped inundation over historical agricultural land.

The alignment-review image is retained locally and not bundled in this repository. Further precision would require better land-cover mapping and independent reference features/imagery.
